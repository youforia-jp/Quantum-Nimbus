How the IQ System Works

We embed secure cryptographic technology directly into hardware, resolving the safety vulnerabilities and supply-chain opacity of conventional 510-thread metallic batteries.

1

Advanced Ceramic Substrate

Circuits are printed directly onto stable ceramic materials, ensuring perfect signal transparency and eliminating the intermittent connection failures common to standard metallic threads.

2

Secure Hardware Verification

An embedded security key verifies cartridge authenticity upon connection using HMAC-SHA256 cryptography — with zero internet dependency or companion applications required.

3

Automatic Thermal Safety Governance

Unverified or counterfeit cartridges trigger an automatic temperature restriction to a 150°C hard ceiling — preventing toxic chemical formation while maintaining minimal device utility.
