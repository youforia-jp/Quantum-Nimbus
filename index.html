How It Works

We build secure, cryptographic technology directly into the hardware, solving the safety risks and vulnerabilities of standard 510-thread metallic batteries.

Advanced Ceramic Hardware

Our circuits are printed directly onto stable ceramic materials. This ensures perfect signal transparency and eliminates the connection issues you get with standard metallic threads.
