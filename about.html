I'm a CS student and embedded systems engineer who builds at the intersection of hardware, firmware, and product — turning low-level BLE telemetry and NFC cryptography into real, shippable user experiences.

Quantum Nimbus is my proof-of-concept for a patent-pending smart vaporization platform: a full-stack project spanning C++ firmware on STM32 microcontrollers, HMAC-SHA256 cartridge authentication, a live Python simulator, and a zero-framework PWA that talks to physical hardware via the Web Bluetooth API.

I don't believe in minimum viable products — every layer of this system was engineered from scratch with zero dynamic allocation, explicit buffer safety, and a product-grade UI that doesn't feel like a prototype.

Open to roles in embedded systems, hardware-adjacent software, or any team that cares deeply about the gap between the silicon and the screen.
