# Quantum Nimbus - Immediate Action Plan

To move Project Nimbus from concept to reality, the following actionable steps should be prioritized.

## 1. Prototype Development & Engineering
- **Hardware (Daniel):** Finalize 3D models for the "Texas Nimbus" and the universal 510-thread chamber. Print a physical V2 mockup to verify cartridge fit and component layout.
- **Systems/App (Sean & Brendon):** Since the complex AI chemical identification is heavily resource-dependent, focus initially on the **Nimbus IQ v3 (App-Centric)** and **NFC Authentication** approach. Build a basic mobile app (React Native/Flutter) that connects to an ESP32 or nRF52-based microcontroller via BLE.
- **Research (Joseph):** Finalize a matrix of optimal temperature curves for CBD, Delta-8, and Delta-9 to be used as default profiles in the app.

## 2. Legal & IP
- **Provisional Patent Application (PPA):** File a PPA focusing on the unique methods: utilizing NFC tags on 510 cartridges for automated optimal heating selection, the "overuse alert" logic, and any novel mechanical designs of the chamber/latch.
- **Operating Agreement:** Execute a formal LLC Operating Agreement detailing equity splits (JP 50%, Daniel 17.5%, etc.), IP assignment to the company, and vesting schedules.
- **Regulatory Strategy:** Consult legal counsel regarding FDA PMTA requirements for the vaporizer and FDA compliance for the "Nimbus Elevate Daily" supplement.

## 3. Supplement Launch (Nimbus Elevate Daily)
- Finalize the formulation (e.g., L-Theanine, Caffeine, B-Vitamins) via a white-label manufacturer.
- Set up a Shopify store and dropshipping logistics.
- Begin the nuanced TikTok marketing strategy focusing on general "Clarity & Focus" and wellness, keeping it separate from the vaporizer hardware regulatory challenges.

## 4. Funding
- Complete the Pitch Deck incorporating the finalized product renders and the dual "Advocate/Innovator" strategy.
- Apply for university grants (Texas A&M) and competitions (Aggie PITCH).
- Prepare to pitch to angel investors or hardware incubators once the functional Proof of Concept (PoC) is ready.
