Quick-Start Guide

Using the Simulator

The Quantum Nimbus simulator is a fully interactive, browser-based demo of the hardware ecosystem — no physical device required. It reproduces the real BLE pairing flow, NFC cartridge authentication, live temperature telemetry, and wellness analytics exactly as they would appear on the production app. Follow the steps below to explore every feature.

01

Connect via WebBLE (or skip to simulation)

Click Scan for Nimbus Device on the phone mockup. If you have a physical Nimbus prototype nearby, your browser will surface it via the Web Bluetooth API — select it and pair. Otherwise, skip directly to Step 2; the simulator panel handles everything offline.

02

Insert a simulated cartridge

In the Simulator Controls panel on the right, click any cartridge button — 🌌 Astro (D9 THC), 🪵 Frontier (Rosin), ☁️ NIMBY (CBD), or ⚠️ Counterfeit. The entire UI — background theme, LED ring color, mascot state, and OLED readout — will update instantly to reflect that cartridge's authentication status.

03

Explore the Control tab — temperature & vaporization profiles

Inside the phone app, the Control tab shows the authenticated cartridge info, three factory presets (Flavor Focus / Balanced / Max Cloud), and a live SVG temperature curve. Toggle Advanced Temperature Control to unlock the fine-tuning slider and watch the graph re-render in real time. Save custom profiles with the + Create New button.

04

Hold to puff & watch live telemetry

Press and hold the PRESS & HOLD TO PUFF button on the device mockup. The OLED temperature climbs to the target, vapor particles animate from the mouthpiece, and the wellness counter increments your milligram intake. Release early to simulate a partial draw — the heater ramps back down accordingly.

05

Check the Wellness tab — NIMBY companion & daily limits

Switch to the Wellness tab to see the animated NIMBY mascot react to your session state (sleeping → happy → angry storm when over limit). The circular progress ring tracks mg consumed today. Adjust your daily limit with the slider, or log a supplement intake to raise your threshold and shift NIMBY into sunny mode.

06

Test counterfeit detection & security override

Insert the ⚠️ Counterfeit cartridge. A warning modal fires, NIMBY goes toxic, and the heater is clamped to 150°C max. In the Developer Options section, toggle Allow Unverified Cartridges or paste a custom NFC JSON payload into the scan field to test your own HMAC-SHA256 signature against the master signing secret.

07

Reset & repeat

Hit 🔄 Reset Demo / Wellness Data at the bottom of the Simulator Controls panel to wipe the session — daily puff count, history chart, and supplement state all clear. Great for running a fresh demo for a recruiter or investor without reloading the tab.
